# Meridian Retail Group: Third-Party Vendor Risk Assessment

## Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Portfolio Simulation

### Overview

This project is a fictional third-party vendor risk assessment developed to demonstrate practical experience in vendor security evaluation, risk scoring, control gap analysis, and executive risk reporting.

Meridian Retail Group is a mid-size e-commerce retailer that expanded its vendor ecosystem to support growth, engaging vendors for payment processing, cloud hosting, AI-powered customer support, marketing analytics, payroll, and logistics. This assessment evaluates each vendor's data access, security posture, and control maturity to identify and prioritize third-party risk.

---

## My Role

**GRC Analyst (Portfolio Simulation)**

Performed vendor risk assessments, scored risk using a Likelihood x Impact model, documented control gaps against industry frameworks, developed a risk treatment plan, and prepared an executive-level report supporting risk-based vendor management decisions.

## Key Risks Identified

- Expired SOC 2 report and weak access controls at the payment processing vendor
- Complete absence of a returned security questionnaire from the logistics vendor
- Indefinite retention of customer PII in AI chatbot transcripts with no data lifecycle policy
- Unmasked production customer data used in a marketing analytics vendor's test environment
- Unassessed fourth-party subcontractor at the cloud hosting vendor

## Business Scenario

Meridian Retail Group conducted its annual third-party risk management cycle across six active vendors with access to company systems or data. The assessment includes vendor inventory, risk scoring, control gap identification, treatment planning, and executive reporting to support informed, risk-based vendor management decisions.
